**Description** (4000)
TV Remote Free turns your phone into a remote for the smart TVs in your home. It's completely free and open source, with no ads, no subscriptions, no accounts and no tracking. It talks only to the TVs on your Wi-Fi.

WORKS WITH
• Roku TVs and players
• Samsung smart TVs (2016 and later)
• LG webOS TVs
• Google TV and Android TV, including Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips and Sharp TVs that run Google TV
No TV handy? Try the built-in demo TV.

A REAL REMOTE
• D-pad, OK, back, home, power, volume and channels
• Shows only the buttons your TV actually supports
• Hold to repeat, with haptic feedback
• Now Playing with progress and playback controls (where the TV reports it)
• Turn the TV on with Wake-on-LAN where supported

TYPE WITH YOUR PHONE
• Use your phone keyboard to search on the TV
• Opens automatically when the TV asks for text (on supported TVs)
• Edit, clear and paste, with an optional search history

TOUCHPAD
• Move a pointer, click and scroll in the TV's web browser

APPS AND WATCHLIST
• Open the TV's apps with one tap
• Save movies, shows and videos from links and open them on the TV later

MAKE IT YOURS
• Themes, including a dark Midnight OLED theme and High Contrast
• Build your own remote layouts, macros and custom buttons
• Household profiles with a simple remote and volume limit for kids
• Left-handed layout, and screen-reader labels on every button

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your phone. Pairing keys are kept in the iPhone Keychain. The app never contacts the internet.

Your phone and TV need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. Some features depend on what your TV supports.

Roku, Samsung, LG, webOS, Google TV and Android TV are trademarks of their respective owners. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

**What's new** (version 1.0.0)
First release. Remote, keyboard, touchpad, apps, watchlist, themes and custom remotes for Roku, Samsung, LG and Google TV.
