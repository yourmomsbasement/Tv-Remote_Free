# Privacy policy — TV Remote Free

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

TV Remote Free is a free, open-source remote control for smart TVs. It is
built to work entirely on your home network.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking, and it does not send any information to the developers or to any
third party.

## What stays on your phone

To work, the app stores the following **only on your device**:

- The TVs you have connected to (name, model, network address, and, where
  the TV reports it, its network hardware address for Wake-on-LAN).
- Pairing keys issued by your TVs, kept in the phone's secure storage
  (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).
- Your settings, themes, remote layouts, macros, watchlist, household
  profiles (the profile PIN is stored only as a one-way hash), and
  optionally your recent keyboard searches. Password fields are never saved.
- App icons downloaded from your TVs, in the app's cache.

You can delete any of this in the app (Forget a TV, Clear search history,
Clear saved app icons, delete profiles/macros/layouts) or by uninstalling
the app.

## Network use

The app only communicates with TVs on your local network, using each TV's
own remote-control protocol. Text you type on the Keyboard tab is sent to
your TV. The app does not contact the internet.

## Permissions

- **Local network** (iPhone) / network access (Android): to find and
  control your TVs.
- **Vibration** (Android): button feedback.
- Files are only read or written when you choose to import or export a
  theme, layout or watchlist.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
Household profiles let parents limit what this app does; they do not
collect information.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file in the project's
repository: https://github.com/yourmomsbasement/Tv-Remote_Free. Questions
can be raised as an issue there.
